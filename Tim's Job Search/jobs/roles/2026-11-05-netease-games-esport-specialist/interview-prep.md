# Interview prep: Esport Specialist at NetEase Games

## The company

NetEase Games is the games division of NetEase, one of China's largest internet companies. It has had a UK office in Guildford/Surrey since 2019, covering business development and global production, which is why this role is based in the UK despite the China-based dev team link.

The role sits in NetEase's shooting-game portfolio. Their established hero shooter is Marvel Rivals (NetEase and Marvel Games, a 6v6 team-based PvP shooter, still being actively updated through 2026). They have also just announced ECC: Redline, a tactical FPS with a Call of Duty: Warzone-style battle royale mode, revealed in October 2026 after a closed playtest. This role likely exists to build out tournament operations for one or both of these shooter titles as NetEase pushes further into competitive/esports support for them, bridging the China-based development teams and a Western esports ecosystem (partners, players, community tournaments).

This is a "Specialist" individual-contributor role, not a leadership position: the brief is hands-on tournament planning and execution (online and offline), partnership and player-relationship management, and coordinating incentive policy with marketing to hit KPIs.

## Likely questions

1. **"Tell us about your experience running esports tournaments."**
   Situation/what he did/result: Head of Esports Operations at BLAST, leading a six-person department running tournament and player operations across CS2, Dota 2, Rocket League, Fortnite, Rainbow Six and Brawl Stars. Result: 60+ flagship events and 300+ qualifiers in 13 countries over six years.

2. **"Do you have experience with shooting-game tournaments specifically?"**
   CS:GO/CS2 as Tournament Operations Manager (2020-2023): managed tournament equipment and staffing budgets on CS:GO specifically, and was the main tournament-level contact for FACEIT, ESIC and BLAST Premier teams. This is the closest direct match to NetEase's shooter titles.

3. **"How do you manage relationships with players and partners?"**
   Brought Team Operations into his department so all online and offline player management and support sat with one team at BLAST. Also managed freelancer and vendor relationships at scale (2,000+ freelancers), and was the main point of contact for FACEIT/ESIC/BLAST Premier.

4. **"How do you coordinate tournament planning with marketing or other teams to hit KPIs?"**
   Costed and pitched new tournament work with product and commercial teams at BLAST, and set technical requirements for commercial partnerships. Honest framing: this was cross-functional coordination at a budget-owner/strategic level, not hands-on administration of an incentive scheme, so be ready to say what he'd need to learn on the operational detail.

5. **"Tell us about running both online and offline tournaments."**
   BLAST events spanned onsite flagship events and online qualifiers (300+ qualifiers) across 13 countries; also booked freelancers for "internal, external and online productions" as Tournament Operations Manager.

6. **"Why are you interested in a specialist/individual-contributor role after being Head of Department?"**
   Answer honestly: interest in the esports sector and this type of tournament operations work, comfortable being hands-on again, and transparent that the step is a deliberate choice for this role rather than an oversight.

7. **"You don't have Chinese language skills listed - how would you work with China-based dev teams?"**
   Be straightforward: no Chinese proficiency currently. Point to experience working cross-border and cross-culture already (13 countries, international freelancers and partners, BLAST Premier's own global structure) and ask how the role typically bridges this (translators, English-speaking liaisons, etc.).

8. **"How do you build or grow a tournament ecosystem?"**
   Rebuilt how his BLAST department worked (ownership, accountability, documentation) and built the department's own tools for tournament operations, event/equipment tracking, bookings and budgets - evidence of building and improving an operational ecosystem, even if not "community ecosystem" in the external partner sense this role means.

9. **"Tell us about a time you managed a tournament budget or incentive-related spend."**
   Managed tournament equipment and staffing budgets across CS:GO, Dota 2, Apex Legends, FIFA, Fortnite and Rainbow Six; approved equipment and staffing invoices; decided when to hire, buy or repair kit. At department level, wrote the business case for a departmental OPEX budget.

10. **"What do you know about our shooting-game titles / esports plans?"**
    Reference Marvel Rivals (NetEase/Marvel hero shooter, actively updated through 2026) and the newly announced ECC: Redline tactical FPS (revealed October 2026), and ask directly which title(s) this role supports.
