- Led a six-person department and restructured it with clear ownership and documentation: use for team leadership (the role has 3 direct reports).
- Managed equipment and staffing budgets, approved invoices, decided when to hire, buy or repair, and wrote the OPEX business case: use for budgeting and forecasting.
- Built in-house tools for bookings, contracts, equipment tracking, freelancer calendars, budgets and deadlines (Airtable): use for systems and data.
- Booked and contracted freelancers and paid them, bought equipment, managed suppliers: use for procurement, inventory and central resources.
- Designated Safeguarding Lead (2021) and first aider at live events: use for safeguarding, important with a mostly child customer base.
- 60+ flagship events and 300+ qualifiers in 13 countries, EUR 12m+ in budgets: use for scale and detail.
- Be upfront on gaps: no monthly P&L/MI reporting to a director, no high-volume billing and collections, no UK GDPR lead role, no Power BI.
