## Likely questions
1. Tell us about yourself. Plan: 15 years in live events and esports; Head of Esports Operations at BLAST, six-person department; 60+ flagship events, 13 countries; now looking to run team operations in a franchise sport.
2. How would you build team operations from scratch? Plan: at BLAST I rebuilt the department with clear ownership, accountability and documentation, and brought Team Operations in so player support sat with one team. Result: one consistent way of working at every event.
3. How do you handle player, agent and owner relationships? Plan: main tournament-level contact for BLAST Premier teams, FACEIT and ESIC; arranged bootcamp venues and equipment. Be honest that agents and owners at franchise level are a step up.
4. What is a shared-services model, and how would you set one up? Plan: centralised freelancer booking, contracting, equipment tracking and budgets that serve every title, built as in-house tools. Frame as the nearest example.
5. How would you set KPIs and report to investors? Plan: I built tools tracking events, equipment, budgets and deadlines and wrote the OPEX business case. Say you have not reported to investors, and you would start from what the owners need to see.
6. Describe managing a large budget. Plan: EUR 12m+ across six years; approved invoices; decided hire, buy or repair; costed new tournament work with product and commercial.
7. How do you cope with travel and event schedules? Plan: 60+ flagship events and 300+ qualifiers across 13 countries; ask them what the travel pattern is.
8. How would you contribute to sponsorship and fan experience? Plan: set technical requirements for commercial partnerships; worked in production on-site for 15 years; honest that sales is not my background.
9. Tell us about a time something went wrong at an event. Plan: pick a real BLAST example Tim chooses (not in the bank; Tim to prepare). Situation, action, result.
10. Why padel/why us? Plan: genuine interest in building a new league; admit no padel background and show you have read up on the sport.
