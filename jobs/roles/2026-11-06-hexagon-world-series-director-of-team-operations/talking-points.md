# Key talking points: Director of Team Operations at Hexagon World Series

- Led a six-person department and brought Team Operations in so online and offline player support sat with one team: use for "how do you build and run a team operation".
- Main tournament-level contact for BLAST Premier teams, FACEIT and ESIC, and arranged venues and equipment for team bootcamps: use for player, team and governing-body relationships.
- Rebuilt how the department works with clear ownership, accountability and documentation: use for shared services, governance and process building.
- Built in-house tools for tournament ops, event and equipment tracking, freelancer calendars, contracts, and event budgets and deadlines: use for scalable systems and reporting.
- 60+ flagship events and 300+ qualifiers in 13 countries, 2,000+ freelancers, EUR 12m+ in budgets: use for scale and global travel around event schedules.
- Costed and pitched new tournament work with product and commercial teams; wrote the business case for a departmental OPEX budget; set technical requirements for commercial partnerships: use for the commercial side, without claiming revenue ownership.
- Be upfront on gaps: no revenue or sponsorship sales ownership, no player contract negotiation, no padel background. Show appetite to learn fast.
