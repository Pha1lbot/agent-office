# Key talking points: Esport Specialist at NetEase Games

- Ran tournament and player operations across every BLAST title (CS2, Dota 2, Rocket League, Fortnite, Rainbow Six, Brawl Stars) as Head of Esports Operations: use for "tell us about tournament operations breadth."
- CS:GO/CS2 is a direct shooting-title match: as Tournament Operations Manager (2020-2023) he managed tournament equipment, staffing budgets and was the main tournament-level contact for FACEIT, ESIC and BLAST Premier teams on that title specifically: use for "do you have shooting-game tournament experience."
- Brought Team Operations into his department so all online and offline player management and support sat with one team: use for "how do you manage player/partner relationships."
- Delivered 60+ flagship events and 300+ qualifiers across 13 countries, both online and offline: use for "experience running tournaments at scale, online and offline."
- Wrote the business case for a departmental OPEX budget, costed and pitched new tournament work with product and commercial teams, and set technical requirements for commercial partnerships: use for "experience with incentive/commercial structures and partnerships," while being honest this was at a strategic/budget-owner level, not hands-on policy administration.
- Booked and managed 2,000+ freelancers and vendors across online and offline productions, including bootcamp venues and equipment: use for "coordinating with marketing/other teams to hit operational deadlines."
- Designed and built his department's own tools for tournament operations, event tracking, bookings and budgets: use to show he can run and improve an ecosystem/process, relevant to "build tournament ecosystems."

## Suggestions from Cole

- Be ready to speak plainly to the two gaps that will come up early: seniority (this is a specialist/IC role; he has been running a department) and Chinese language (he has none). Don't let these surface as surprises - name them himself in the first few minutes if the interviewer doesn't raise them, framed as deliberate: he's looking at this role for the sector and title fit, happy to operate as an individual contributor again.
- Use the CS2 FACEIT/ESIC/BLAST Premier contact experience as the closest concrete answer to "shooting game tournament experience," since it's the most specific match in the CV bank to NetEase's shooter titles (Marvel Rivals, ECC: Redline).
- Keep numbers (60+ events, 2,000+ freelancers, €12m+) in reserve rather than leading with them: pitched at Head of Department level, they may underline the seniority mismatch rather than help, unless the interviewer asks about scale directly.
